I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, co-advised by [Bilge Mutlu](https://bmutlu.github.io/) and [Josiah Hanna](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~jphanna/). I received my Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan–Ann Arbor (2022–2023), advised by [Christoforos Mavrogiannis](https://chrismavrogiannis.com/).

My research focuses on **Embodied AI** and **Robot Learning**, with an emphasis on dexterous manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, vision-language-actionm and world models. I am particularly interested in enabling robots to perform complex physical tasks in unstructured real-world environments.
