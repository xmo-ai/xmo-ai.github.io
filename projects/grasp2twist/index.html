Grasp2Twist: Learning Bimanual Dexterous Jar Opening by Reinforcement Learning

Bimanual dexterous jar opening with a single RL policy, transferred zero-shot to hardware

Mo Xu1, Yunfu Deng1, Jianuo Wang2, Josiah Hanna1†, Bilge Mutlu1†

1University of Wisconsin-Madison   2Independent Researcher   †Joint senior authors

arXiv Code

One RL-trained policy grasps and twists open jars with a bimanual dexterous system.

One hand reconfigures finger-object contacts to enable sustained lid rotation while the other hand holds the jar.

Task and Method

Physical system (left) and simulation (right). The holding hand grasps and stabilizes the jar; the twisting hand grasps and twists the lid by reconfiguring contacts.

We guide the fingers to a spatial arrangement during grasping and signal the grasp-to-twist transition.

We train the policy with a three-stage curriculum.

STAGE 1

Foundational skill acquisition

Learn grasp acquisition and the grasp-to-twist transition.

STAGE 2

Contact reconfiguration exploration

Explore finger contact changes for sustained twisting.

STAGE 3

Sim-to-real adaptation

Build sim-to-real robustness with domain randomization.
